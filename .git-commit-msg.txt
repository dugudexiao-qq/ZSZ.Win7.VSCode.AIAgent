v1.11.2: sanitize imported HTML into plain text

- HTML/HTM imports are converted to readable text before entering the
  context: scripts, styles, comments and tags are dropped, <title> becomes
  a heading, block tags become line breaks, entities are decoded
- Previously raw markup was injected (up to 60000 chars), which made small
  models treat tags as content and script bodies as instructions
- .htm and upper-case extensions are now accepted; unknown binary types
  are refused with a message that explains why
